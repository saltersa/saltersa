## THE FOUR GATES

Fixed points where the campaign continues or changes. Set now, because in the moment everyone
argues for continuing.

| Gate | When | Test | If it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | End of week 2 | 8 or more of 17 clerks replied | Contact data is wrong. Stop sending, re verify, do not burn the list. |
| 2 | End of week 6 | At least one county meeting on the calendar | County angle is not landing. Escalate to a CEO letter or a board introduction. |
| 3 | End of week 8 | 3 or more towns discussing a specific parcel | Open Tier 3 and push the church channel hard. |
| 4 | End of week 12 | 1 site in written negotiation | Trigger the market purchase fallback and reset the 2027 date. |

## THE WEEKLY REVIEW

Thirty minutes, same slot every week, Antonio chairs. Five numbers, one decision.

1. Touches sent, against plan.
2. Replies, and the running reply rate.
3. Meetings booked.
4. Named parcels or buildings in play.
5. Days until the nearest funding cycle closes.
6. **The decision: which town or county gets dropped this week.**

That last item is the one that matters. Campaigns fail by spreading thin across contacts who
are all "still warm." Kill the dead ones and move the hours to the live ones.
