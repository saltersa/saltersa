# Phone Scripts

Two scripts. A 30 second live call and a voicemail under 20 seconds. Read them out loud
once before dialing. Do not improvise extra facts on the phone.

---

## LIVE CALL, 30 SECONDS

> Good morning, this is [NAME] from Spectrum for Living.
>
> We run group homes for people with disabilities in Bergen, Passaic, and Middlesex
> counties, including one right here in [TOWN]. We have been doing it since 1986.
>
> I am calling about surplus town property. We are planning a home for adults who need
> nursing care around the clock, and we need a site.
>
> Is there 20 minutes on your calendar in the next two weeks?

Roughly 80 words, which runs about 30 seconds at a normal pace.

**The only ask is the 20 minutes.** Do not pitch the project on this call. Do not name a
parcel. Get the meeting and get off the phone.

---

## VOICEMAIL, UNDER 20 SECONDS

> Hi, this is [NAME] at Spectrum for Living, [PHONE NUMBER].
>
> We run a group home in [TOWN], and we are looking for surplus town property for a new
> one. I would like 20 minutes of your time.
>
> My number again is [PHONE NUMBER]. Thank you.

Roughly 48 words, which runs about 18 seconds.

**Say the phone number twice, slowly.** It is the only thing they have to write down.

---

## VARIANTS BY TARGET TYPE

**Township or borough.** Use as written above.

**County office.** Replace the second and third lines with:

> We serve about 600 people a year across Bergen, Passaic, and Middlesex counties.
>
> I am calling about your CDBG and HOME funding, and how special needs units get built.

**Housing authority.** Replace the second and third lines with:

> We house adults who need nursing care 24 hours a day, the applicants that standard
> accessible units cannot serve.
>
> I am calling about set asides and rehab partnerships.

---

## IF A GATEKEEPER ANSWERS

> I am trying to reach the person who handles [surplus municipal property / CDBG and HOME
> funding / accessible unit set asides]. Who would that be?

Take the name and the correct spelling. Log it in `sources.md` with how you got it, then
confirm it against the official website before writing to that person. A name given over
the phone is a lead, not a verified contact.

---

## IF THEY ASK A QUESTION YOU CANNOT ANSWER

Say so and follow up in writing.

> I do not want to give you a wrong number on that. Let me confirm it and email you today.

Never estimate a figure on a call.

---

## FILL BEFORE DIALING

- `[NAME]` and `[PHONE NUMBER]`: [ASK ANTONIO: who makes these calls, and from what
  number?]
- `[TOWN]`. The "one right here in [TOWN]" line is true only for Bergenfield, Edison,
  Westwood, Elmwood Park, and Paramus. Cut it for any other town.
