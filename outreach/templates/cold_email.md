# Cold Email Templates

Three variants: township, county office, housing authority. Each body is under 150 words.
Each opens with the recipient's problem, not with us. Each ends with one ask, a 20 minute
call.

---

## STOP: TWO CLAIMS MUST BE VERIFIED BEFORE ANY OF THESE SEND

**1. Town specific obligation and trust fund status.** The township variant refers to the
town's Fourth Round obligation and its trust fund. The campaign brief requires that this be
confirmed for each individual town before the angle is used with that town. It has not been
confirmed for any town. Fill the bracket or cut the sentence.

**2. Whether Spectrum's units generate affordable housing credit.** The strongest version
of this pitch says a group home helps a town meet its obligation. That is a program and
legal rule, and campaign rule 2 forbids asserting it without a source. **No source has been
obtained.** No variant below asserts it. Do not add it until it is verified and logged in
`sources.md`.

**3. The "we already run a group home in [TOWN]" line** is true only for Bergenfield,
Edison, Westwood, Elmwood Park, and Paramus. Cut it for any other town until the full
property list is confirmed.

Spectrum for Living has served people with developmental and physical disabilities in New
