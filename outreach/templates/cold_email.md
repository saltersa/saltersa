# Cold Email Templates

Four variants: township, county office, housing authority, and leased day program site.
Each body is under 150 words. Each opens with the recipient's problem, not with us. Each
ends with one ask, a 20 minute call.

**Signer:** Elizabeth Boyajian, Director of Development, Marketing, and Public Relations.
**Approver:** Antonio. Nothing sends without his explicit approval.

---

## STOP: VERIFY BEFORE ANY OF THESE SEND

**1. Town specific obligation and trust fund status.** Variant A refers to the town's Fourth
Round obligation and its trust fund. This must be confirmed for each individual town before
the angle is used with that town. It has not been confirmed for any town. Fill the bracket
or cut the sentence.

**2. Whether Spectrum's units generate affordable housing credit.** The strongest version of
this pitch says a group home helps a town meet its obligation. That is a program and legal
rule, and campaign rule 2 forbids asserting it without a source. **No source has been
obtained.** No variant below asserts it. Do not add it until it is verified and logged.

**3. Town facts come from `/outreach/facilities.md` only.** The "since [YEAR]" line and the
facility names are in that file, computed from the 2026 facility list. Spectrum operates in
18 municipalities. Do not write to a nineteenth town using these templates.

**4. Variant D discloses aggregate rent.** [ASK ANTONIO: are you comfortable telling a town
that Spectrum pays $451,908 a year in rent across its three leased day program sites? It
proves we are a paying tenant rather than a charity case, but it also shows a town what we
can afford. Per site figures are deliberately not disclosed.]

## VARIANT B: COUNTY OFFICE

**Subject:** 20 minutes about CDBG and special needs housing

Dear [TITLE] [LAST NAME],

[COUNTY] puts CDBG and HOME dollars to work every year. Special needs units are among the
hardest to source. Plenty of applicants can build. Far fewer can operate housing for adults
who need nursing care around the clock.

We do both.

Spectrum for Living has served people with developmental and physical disabilities in New
Jersey since 1986. We run [N] facilities in [N] [COUNTY] municipalities. We handle public
money already, including $187,475 in New Jersey Department of Labor grants.

Every person we house is low or moderate income and Medicaid funded.

We are planning a $1 million group home for medically fragile adults, targeted for 2027.

Could we have 20 minutes?

Thank you,

[SIGNATURE BLOCK]

## VARIANT C: HOUSING AUTHORITY

**Subject:** 20 minutes about your hardest to place applicants

Dear [TITLE] [LAST NAME],

Some applicants need more than a standard accessible unit can give them. Adults who need
nursing care 24 hours a day are the clearest case. Those are the people we house.

Spectrum for Living has done this in New Jersey since 1986. We serve about 600 people a year
and run 27 facilities across 18 municipalities. We run group homes, supervised apartments,
and an intermediate care facility with 24 hour skilled nursing.

Every person we house is low or moderate income and Medicaid funded.

We are interested in accessible set asides, rehabs of existing stock, and co-development.

Could we have 20 minutes to talk about the applicants you cannot place?

Thank you,

[SIGNATURE BLOCK]

## VARIANT D: LEASED DAY PROGRAM SITE

**Use only for Westwood, Teaneck, and North Haledon.** These are the three towns where
Spectrum rents rather than owns. Check `/outreach/facilities.md` before sending, and read
the Westwood lease flag there first.

**Subject:** 20 minutes about our [TOWN] day program

Dear [TITLE] [LAST NAME],

Spectrum for Living has run a day program in [TOWN] since [YEAR]. We do not own the
building. We rent it.

Across our three rented day program sites we pay $451,908 a year in rent. That is money
leaving our programs. We would rather spend it on services, and we would rather be a
permanent part of [TOWN].

Everywhere else we operate, we own. We own 23 of our 27 buildings, in 18 New Jersey
municipalities, going back to 1986.

If [TOWN] owns a building that is underused, would you give me 20 minutes to talk about it?

Thank you,

[SIGNATURE BLOCK]
