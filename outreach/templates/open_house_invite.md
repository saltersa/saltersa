# Open House Invitation

Phase 4, Month 1. Also the strongest conversion tool in the campaign.

An official who walks through a working Spectrum facility understands in ten minutes what no
email conveys. Some people will never answer an email and will absolutely turn up to
something in their own county.

---

## USE THE CLOSTER INTERMEDIATE CARE FACILITY

50 Blanch Avenue, Closter. Open since 1983, renovated 2010. 29,500 square feet, 60 beds, 24
hour skilled nursing. Owned by Spectrum.

It is the right venue for three reasons.

1. **It is the proof.** The campaign's central claim is that Spectrum can operate care almost
   nobody else can. This building is that claim, standing up, for 43 years.
2. **It answers the property value question without arguing.** They are standing in a
   residential Bergen County town where Spectrum has been the neighbour since 1983.
3. **It is in Bergen County**, where 14 of the 18 towns and 19 of the 27 facilities are.

> residential facility? Resident privacy and dignity come first, and you know the constraints
> I do not. If Closter is wrong, the same invitation works for any site you choose.

**Subject:** An invitation to see what we do, [DATE]

Dear [TITLE] [LAST NAME],

We have been part of [COUNTY] for 40 years, and most people have never seen inside one of our
buildings.

I would like to change that. On [DATE] at [TIME] we are opening our Closter facility to
elected officials and county staff. It has been open since 1983. Sixty beds, skilled nursing
around the clock, for adults who cannot be cared for anywhere else.

It takes about an hour. You will meet the people who live there and the nurses who look after
them. There is no presentation and no ask.

We are planning a new home for medically fragile adults, and towns across [COUNTY] will hear
from us about it. I would rather you saw the work first.

Can you join us?

[SIGNATURE BLOCK]

---

## WHY IT IS WRITTEN THIS WAY

**"There is no presentation and no ask."** Say it and mean it. The visit is the persuasion.
An official who feels pitched at a site visit will not come to the second one, and will warn
colleagues.

**"I would rather you saw the work first."** This reframes the whole campaign. You are not
asking for something before they know you. You are showing them first. That is a status
reversal, and it makes the later ask land differently.

**"Forty years."** Incorporated 1986. In Closter since 1983.

**No dollar figures.** Not the budget, not the rent, not the $1 million. Wrong tone for an
invitation.

## WHO TO INVITE

In this order:

1. Every Band 2 and Band 5 contact who replied to anything, even a no. **Especially the no.**
   A no on paper is not a no in person.
2. Bergen County community development, human services, and planning.
3. Mayors and administrators of the Bergen towns hosting a facility. Fourteen towns.
4. The Spectrum clients already serving on Bergen County advisory boards, and the county staff
   who support them. That relationship is already funded and already warm.
5. Columbia Bank and the Chinery Foundation. Funders in the room raise the temperature for
   officials, and it doubles as Phase 4 Month 2 relationship work.

**Send it four weeks out, with a reminder one week out.** Municipal calendars fill early.
