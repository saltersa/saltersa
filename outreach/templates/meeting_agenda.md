# 20 Minute Meeting Agenda

Send this to the contact the day before. It signals that you will respect the clock, and it
makes the three asks visible before you walk in.

---

## THE SHAPE OF THE MEETING

Talk for eight minutes. Listen for seven. Ask for five. Most people run this backwards.

---

### 0:00 to 0:02, Thanks and purpose

> Thank you for the time. I want to use 20 minutes and give it back to you. Here is what I
> am hoping to leave with, and then I mostly want to hear about your situation.

State the three asks up front. Then stop.

### 0:02 to 0:05, Who Spectrum is

Three sentences, no more.

> Spectrum for Living has served people with developmental and physical disabilities in
> New Jersey since 1986. We serve about 600 people a year across Bergen, Passaic, and
> Middlesex counties, on a budget of about $48.9 million, with a 4 star Charity Navigator
> rating. We run group homes, supervised apartments, and an intermediate care facility with
> 24 hour skilled nursing.

If they already host a Spectrum home, add one line: we have been your neighbor in [TOWN]
for [ASK ANTONIO: how long has each home been open in Bergenfield, Edison, Westwood,
Elmwood Park, and Paramus?].

### 0:05 to 0:12, Their situation, listen

This is the part that decides whether there is a second meeting. Ask, then write down what
they say.

- What is your obligation under the Fourth Round, and where does it stand today?
- What is sitting in the affordable housing trust fund, and what is the plan for it?
- What property does the town own that is not doing anything?
- What has stopped past projects here? Neighbors, cost, or something else?
- Who else needs to be in the room for this to move?

Do not correct them. Do not pitch over them.

### 0:12 to 0:15, The project

> We are building a group home for adults who are medically fragile and need nursing care
> 24 hours a day. About $1 million. We are targeting 2027.
>
> Every person we house is low or moderate income and Medicaid funded. That is 100 percent
> of them.
>
> Very few organizations in New Jersey can operate housing at this level of care. We
> already do.

### 0:15 to 0:20, The three asks

Ask all three. Do not soften them. Write the answers down in front of them.

---

## THE THREE CLOSING ASKS

**1. Land.** Will you send me the list of properties the town owns and is not using? And
will you walk the two or three most promising ones with me?

**2. A partner on your side.** Who on your staff should I work with from here, and may I
tell them you sent me?

**3. Money.** Will you consider this project when the trust fund spending plan is written
this year? Or, for a county: will you consider it in the next CDBG and HOME round?

---

## BEFORE YOU LEAVE THE ROOM

Get a date. Not "we will be in touch." An actual next date on an actual calendar.

> Can we put 30 minutes on the calendar for [DATE] to go through the property list?

---

## AFTER

Thank you email within 24 hours, with the agreed next steps written out. Draft it and send
it to Antonio for approval first. Campaign rule: nothing goes out without approval.

---

## VARIANTS BY TARGET TYPE

**Township or borough.** Use as written.

**County office.** In the listening section, replace the first two questions with: how is
the CDBG and HOME allocation committed this cycle, and what does the application timeline
look like? In ask 3, name CDBG and HOME instead of the trust fund.

**Housing authority.** In the listening section, ask: how many applicants on your list need
more care than an accessible unit provides, and what does your rehab pipeline look like? In
ask 1, ask about units and rehab candidates rather than land.
