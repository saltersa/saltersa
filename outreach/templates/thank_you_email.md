# Thank You Email

Sent **within 24 hours** of every meeting. Required by the campaign brief, Phase 3.

This is not a courtesy. It is the record. Whatever is written down within a day becomes what
happened in the meeting, and a vague thank you throws away the only leverage a meeting
produces.

Drafted by whoever took the meeting, approved by Antonio, sent same day or next morning.

---

## THE EMAIL

**Subject:** Thank you, and what we agreed

Dear [TITLE] [LAST NAME],

Thank you for the time this morning. I want to write down what I heard, so we are working
from the same page.

**What you told me**

- [Their obligation or trust fund position, in their words]
- [What property exists, or does not]
- [What has blocked projects here before]

**What we agreed**

1. [Action] by [who] by [date]
2. [Action] by [who] by [date]
3. [Action] by [who] by [date]

**What I owe you**

[The thing you promised in the room. Send it with this email if you can.]

If I have any of that wrong, tell me and I will correct it.

Next step is [the specific next thing] on [date]. I have it in my calendar.

Thank you again,

[SIGNATURE BLOCK]

---

## RULES

**Send it within 24 hours.** After that it reads as an afterthought and the details have
already drifted.

**Write what they said, not what you wish they had said.** If they were lukewarm, the email
says lukewarm. An official who reads back an inflated version of their own position gets
defensive and slower.

**Every action has a name and a date.** "We will follow up" is not an action. "I will send
the surplus parcel list by Friday" is.

**Attach what you promised.** If you said you would send the capability one pager, attach it
here rather than in a second email. One email, one follow up, done.

**If nothing was agreed, say so.** Then:

> I know we did not land on a next step. If it is useful, I can send [the specific thing] and
> come back to you in [timeframe]. If this is not a fit for [TOWN], that is a fine answer and
> I would rather know.

That paragraph converts a dead meeting into either a real no or a second chance. Both beat
drift.

**Log it in `tracker.csv` the same day.** Reply status, next action, next action date.
