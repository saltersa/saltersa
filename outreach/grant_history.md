## The finding that should shape the campaign

**Spectrum has never received CDBG, HOME, NJHMFA, DCA, county community development, or
municipal affordable housing trust fund money.** Not once between 2022 and 2027. I searched
every funder name and program note in the file for all of those terms and found nothing.

That is the campaign in one sentence. The entire government housing funding stack is
untouched, and it is exactly the stack the campaign targets.

## Spectrum is not a first time public grantee

This matters, because the first question a county or municipal officer asks is whether an
organization can handle public money and public reporting. Spectrum already does.

Two of these deserve a direct mention in outreach.

- **Inclusive Healthy Communities**, $248,660.00 across 2023 to 2025. The funded project is
  named in the file as "Inclusion Across the Spectrum, Connecting Clients to Positions on
  Bergen County Advisory Boards." Spectrum has already been paid by a state program to put
  the people it serves onto Bergen County advisory boards. That is a county government
  relationship that already exists.
- **NJ Department of Labor**, $187,475.00 across 2025 and 2026 in training grants, with a
  2026 to 2027 consortium grant listed as pending.

Foundation and The Arc of New Jersey contacts already in the grant file."
