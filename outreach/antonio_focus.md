# Antonio's List

You said you will do the major contact and your time has to go to the best contacts. This page
decides which those are, and takes everything else off your desk.

**The principle: your time goes where a relationship changes an outcome.** Nowhere else.
Seventeen near identical records requests do not need a Chief Operating Officer. They need
thirty minutes of your approval and a send button.

---

## THE TWELVE THAT ARE WORTH YOUR TIME

Ranked. If you only do the first four, the campaign still has a real chance.

### 1. Your own facilities staff. Not outreach at all.

**Do this first, today, before any external contact.**

You own 100, 200 and 300 Spectrum Drive in Edison. Is there developable land there, or a
building with convertible space? Same question for River Vale, Ringwood, and Closter.

**Why it outranks everything:** no acquisition cost, no seller, no closing, no town, no
approval. On a $1 million budget that has to cover buying and building, this is the only path
where the whole million goes into the building. **It could end the search in one phone call.**

### 2. The church that owns the North Haledon Adult Training Center.

High Mountain Church, Darren Ojeda listed as pastor in your facility file. Spectrum has been in
that building **since 1989**.

**Why:** thirty seven years of landlord relationship, and a church can decide without a council
vote, public notice, or a public hearing. That is the single fastest external decision available
to you. They already know what Spectrum does because they have watched it for nearly four
decades.

**Ask:** do you have other property, or do you know a congregation that does?

### 3. The church that owns the Teaneck Adult Training Center.

Grace Lutheran Church. Spectrum since 2006. Same logic as above, shorter relationship.

### 4. Bergen County, Director of Community Development.

**The warmest door in the campaign and the highest leverage single conversation.**

Spectrum was paid $248,660 through Inclusive Healthy Communities to place the people it serves
onto **Bergen County advisory boards**. You already have a funded working relationship with
this county government. Open with that sentence.

Bergen covers **14 of your 18 towns and 19 of your 27 facilities**. One good meeting here is
worth more than fourteen town emails.

**Two asks, in this order:** when does the CDBG and HOME cycle open and close, and does the
county own a building.

### 5. Edison Township Administrator.

Four facilities since 1997, your largest single town footprint, and the Spectrum Drive campus
sits there. If item 1 turns up land, this person is who you talk to next anyway.

### 6. Middlesex County, Division of Housing, Community Development and Social Services.

Administers CDBG and HOME. Arts Institute of Middlesex County has funded Spectrum five
consecutive years, which is a county affiliated tie you can name.

### 7. Closter Borough Administrator.

**Since 1983. Forty three years.** The intermediate care facility at 50 Blanch Avenue is your
single best proof point in the state, and it is in their town. Nobody is better placed to
confirm what kind of neighbour Spectrum is.

Worth the call even if Closter has no building, because this is the reference you will ask
every other town to check.

### 8 to 12. The multi facility towns.

Bergenfield, River Vale, Glen Rock, Ringwood, Paramus. Two facilities each except Paramus, all
established between 1988 and 2002. Administrator level, not mayor.

## HOW TO SPEND THE TIME YOU DO GIVE IT

**Batch the approvals.** One sitting, seventeen emails, thirty minutes. Not seventeen
interruptions across a week.

**Take every meeting yourself for the twelve above.** Send someone else and you lose the thing
that makes them worth doing.

**Ask every single one the referral question.** It costs one sentence and outperforms everything
else in the campaign:

> Even if this is not for you, who else should I be talking to?

**Kill things weekly.** Thirty minutes, same slot. The important output is not the status
update, it is deciding which town stops getting your attention. Campaigns fail by staying
polite to fifty contacts who are all still warm.

## THE ONE THING TO REMEMBER IN EVERY CONVERSATION

Under **N.J.S.A. 40:55D-66.1**, a community residence for the developmentally disabled housing
six or fewer people is a **permitted use in every residential district in New Jersey**, held to
the same standard as a single family house. Verify with counsel first, and confirm the license
class, both flagged in `schedule.md`.

If it holds, **you are not asking any town for permission.** You are asking for a building.
That is a different posture, and it should show in how you sit in the room. Never say it as a
threat. Its value is that you negotiate from a settled position instead of a hopeful one.
